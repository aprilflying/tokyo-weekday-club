# Everyday Japanese collage

Created for the Tokyo Weekday Club program section on 14 November 2026, using the built-in image generation tool (generate mode). The final transparent PNG is `japanese-everyday-collage.png`; HTML supplies the Japanese, pronunciation and English/Traditional Chinese labels. CSS frames the eight sprite cells and reflows the collage on phones.

Prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a photographic cutout sprite sheet for a playful Tokyo children's Japanese lesson collage. Create ONE transparent PNG canvas, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio, ideally 2048x1024. Arrange EXACTLY eight isolated subject groups in an invisible, perfectly regular 4-column by 2-row grid; each equal square cell contains ONE group centered in its own cell with at least 12% empty transparent margin all around. Nothing may cross between cells. Do not draw the grid or any panel backgrounds. Top row left to right: (1) two overlapping tactile paper speech bubbles, one terracotta red and one blush pink, completely blank; (2) a realistic Japanese triangular onigiri rice ball with dark nori wrap, beside its small crinkled white paper wrapper; (3) a clear tumbler of orange juice with a pale striped paper straw; (4) a charming small fan of red, yellow and blue wooden colouring pencils, red pencil most prominent. Bottom row left to right: (5) a slightly worn classic black-and-white football; (6) a small unbranded wooden toy train in warm red and natural wood; (7) three overlapping ivory square blank paper flashcards with a short red pencil, no writing; (8) a beautiful folded blush-red origami crane. Art direction: sophisticated editorial scrapbook, believable photographed objects and paper texture, subtle cut-paper edges and gentle contact shadows, friendly and warm, not glossy 3D, not clip art, not cartoon characters. Palette harmonizes with warm ivory #FAF7F2, terracotta #C0392B, blush #F8DDD8 and black. All eight objects need comparable scale and visual weight. No people, no hands, no faces, no logos, no letters, no Japanese glyphs, no text anywhere: all labels will be added by the website. Truly transparent background.

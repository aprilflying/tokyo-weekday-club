founder story

Why I built this

創辦人的話

I am a work-from-home mom, and I have been taking my kids to Tokyo for three summers. Every year I look for activities they can really get into: a month with a private Japanese tutor, Japanese classes, painting classes. We found some great ones. I wanted to bring the best of them together for visiting families.我是一位在家工作的媽媽，連續三個暑假都帶小朋友來東京。每一年，我都在找一些真正讓他們投入、又能學到東西的活動：試過為期一個月的私人日語老師、日語班、繪畫班，每一樣都有很好的體驗。我一直在想，如果可以把這些好的體驗集合起來，變成一個專為來東京旅行的家庭而設的課程就好了。所以，我開始把它一步一步做出來。

Starting with Japanese. Small classes, a few hours a day, native Japanese teachers, and people I trust with my own kids. More to come.先由日語開始。小班教學、每天幾個小時、日籍老師，讓小朋友真正有機會在東京學日文、用日文。之後，我們希望慢慢加入更多活動。

I am the customer.因為這正是我自己一直想為小朋友找到的課程。

faq

Questions parents actually ask

家長最常問的問題
ask us anything
My child speaks zero Japanese. Is that OK?小朋友完全不懂日文，可以嗎？+

Perfect, actually. Level 1 starts from zero. By the first pick-up, your child can introduce themselves and say thank you properly.完全沒問題。Level 1 從零開始。第一天放學時，小朋友已經可以用日文自我介紹，也懂得有禮貌地說謝謝。

We're only in Tokyo for a week. Can we join?我們只來東京一個星期，可以嗎？+

Yes. We built this for visiting families, so 1 week is all you need. We're also working on longer programs, including the whole summer.可以。課程本來就是為來東京旅行的家庭而設，1個星期就可以參加。我們也正在設計更長的課程，例如整個暑假。

What's the shortest commitment? And the longest?最短可以報讀多久？最長呢？+

Start with 1 week and extend if your child likes it. 3 weeks complete Level 1, with Levels 2 to 5 in the works.最短1個星期，喜歡的話可以繼續參加。3個星期完成 Level 1，Level 2 至 5 也正在籌備中。

Do you only have one location?目前只有一個上課地點嗎？+

Yes, for now we're in Meguro. We're looking at a second location, possibly in Setagaya. Message us for the latest.是，目前在目黑。我們正在考慮第二個地點，可能會在世田谷。歡迎聯絡我們了解最新消息。

What languages do the teachers speak?老師上課會說甚麼語言？+

Lessons are in Japanese (that's the point), but every teacher speaks English. For parents, we communicate in English or Chinese over WhatsApp.課堂以日語進行（這正是重點），但每位老師都懂英文。與家長則以英文或中文溝通，主要透過 WhatsApp 聯絡。

Can siblings of different ages join?不同年齡的兄弟姊妹可以一起報名嗎？+

Yes. Kids are grouped roughly by age, 6–8 and 9–12, with classes running at the same time. Siblings arrive and leave together.可以。班別大致分為6–8歲和9–12歲，並安排在同一地點、同一時段上課。兄弟姊妹可以一起來、一起放學。

Who runs the program?課程由誰負責？+

Tokyo Weekday Club designs the program around visiting families, with input from MAKKAM Language Room. Every class is taught by MAKKAM's Japanese teachers. We each do what we do best.Tokyo Weekday Club 按照旅遊家庭的需要設計課程，並由 MAKKAM Language Room 提供專業教學意見。所有課堂均由 MAKKAM 的日籍老師教授，各自發揮所長。

Is food provided?會提供食物嗎？+

We generally don't provide food or drinks because of allergies. Some classes may involve making food together, and we'll always ask you first. Kids are welcome to bring a snack and water bottle for breaks.考慮到食物過敏，我們一般不提供食物或飲品。部分課堂可能會一起製作簡單食物，我們一定會事先徵求家長同意。小朋友可以自備小食和水，小息時享用。

How do I pay from overseas?海外家長如何付款？+

Save a card when you book through Stripe (Visa, Mastercard or AMEX). Nothing is charged that day. Once your class is confirmed, we charge in Japanese yen and your card handles the conversion.預約時透過 Stripe 網上儲存信用卡(Visa、Mastercard、AMEX)，當日不會扣款。班別確認開班後，我們以日圓收費，匯率換算由你的信用卡處理。

Can I leave my kids longer than 3 hours?可以讓小朋友留超過3小時嗎？+

We hope you'll spend the rest of the day together. It's your holiday too. For longer days, we're talking with a neighbourhood art school our own kids attended about adding a 2-hour art program, making up to 5 hours altogether. Message us for the latest.我們鼓勵家長在假期裡多陪伴小朋友。如果需要更長時間，我們正在與附近一間美術教室洽談（我們自己的小朋友也在那裡上過課），提供2小時的美術課程，加起來一天最多可達5小時。歡迎聯絡我們了解最新消息。

What's the cancellation policy?取消政策是怎樣的?+

Before your week is confirmed, cancel anytime; you haven't been charged yet. Once confirmed, cancellations 14+ days before class receive a 50% refund; less than 14 days and no-shows are non-refundable. Child unwell? Send us a medical certificate and we'll move you to another week for free. Flight problems? We'll do the same.星期確認開班前，可隨時免費取消，因為尚未收費;請使用確認電郵內的取消連結。星期確認並收費後，開課前14天或以上通知可退款50%;少於14天或缺席恕不退款。如小朋友生病，請提供醫生證明，我們會免費改期至另一週;航班問題亦可聯絡我們安排改期。
